# Public marketing claims

Updated October 21, 2026. This is the maintained source for claim provenance and
publication rules. Dated receipts describe earlier copy; they are not proof of
current performance or customer approval.

| Claim | Evidence and publication rule |
| --- | --- |
| Analyzed-video total | The owner manually tracks lifetime Firebase video jobs; older job documents are deleted. `src/app/lib/constants.ts` owns the configured 5,000+ estimate. Homepage, product and partner pages identify it as an owner estimate. A retained-job query cannot reproduce the lifetime total. |
| App Store rating | The shared configured value was checked against the US listing on October 20, 2026. This is a dated listing observation, not a live rating feed; refresh it before changing the value. |
| Analysis capabilities | Scorecards, coaching feedback and saved results describe product behavior. The backend API reference owns the integration contract; website copy does not grant credentials or enable an environment. |
| Turnaround | Current pages, search metadata and the social-image renderer make no specific processing-time promise. Restoring a time claim requires dated end-to-end measurements, sample size, input/network conditions and an appropriate range. |
| Customer sandbox | Availability has not been established. The partner page invites a discussion of integration testing; it does not promise an available customer sandbox. |
| Experience and biographies | Current biographies describe contributor roles without unverified career-duration, audience-size, award or league-history claims. Restore specific credentials only with a source and an observation date. |
| Testimonials | Named customer quotations and attached five-star graphics have been removed from current page composition pending source and publication-approval records. Earlier text remains in Git history; its presence there does not establish authenticity or permission. |

Keep legal terms and team access contracts under their existing owners. Direct
team payments, access grants, partner discounts and organization membership remain
distinct, as recorded in the app repository's
[direct-team-sales decision](https://github.com/jakedibattista/puck-buddy/blob/main/docs/decisions/0006-direct-team-sales-and-store-discovery.md).

This review covers current page text, metadata and generated social-image text.
It does not certify old store artwork, archived screenshots or app-store listings.
Changed source must pass website checks and be deployed before it can describe the
public site's observed state.


The maintained social-banner generator also avoids instant-result and unverified
rink-testing copy. Its generated files remain local drafts; previously exported
social images, old store artwork and uploaded social assets require their own
review. Generator-source corrections do not establish replacement or publication.
