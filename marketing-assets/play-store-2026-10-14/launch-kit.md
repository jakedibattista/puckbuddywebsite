# Puck Buddy Android launch kit

Prepared October 14, 2026. Listing changes are saved as a Google Play draft.
Announcement copy is prepared for use after the approved release is available.
No social post, email, advertising campaign or public release has been sent.

## Positioning

Puck Buddy helps hockey players turn practice footage into a clearer focus for
training. Lead with technique review and useful coaching feedback. Progress and
Games support the practice habit; security implementation is not the main pitch.

Campaign line: **Practice. Review. Repeat.**

Store title: **Puck Buddy: Hockey Training**

Short description: **Review hockey videos, get AI coaching feedback and track your practice**

The complete en-US listing is in `listing-en-US.json`. Keep account, premium and
under-13 consent disclosures visible in the full description. Do not promise
instant analysis, guaranteed improvement, calibrated shot speeds, or a historical
video library.

## Artwork sequence

1. **Review every rep.** Actual technique scorecard; the primary product benefit.
2. **Turn feedback into practice.** Actual written feedback, with a caption about planning the next practice.
3. **Build a practice habit.** Actual progress/badge screen.
4. **Make practice a game.** Actual shooting and timed-skating challenge menu.

The feature graphic uses the campaign line and an actual scorecard. All graphics
use the app's navy/blue palette and existing logo. The four screenshots are
1080×1920 RGB PNGs; the feature graphic is 1024×500 RGB PNG. The app icon retains
its existing identity. No invented screens, customer testimonials or staged
improvement claims are included. Capture provenance is held privately.

## Launch announcement draft

Practice. Review. Repeat.

The latest Puck Buddy Android update makes it easier to review your hockey
practice: clearer scorecards, coaching feedback you can read or listen to, and
practice stats that help you follow your progress.

Record a short drill clip, review the result and choose one thing to work on next.
You can also try shooting and skating challenges and join your team with an
invitation code.

AI feedback and speed estimates vary with the recording. Follow the filming tips
in the app for useful results. Some features require premium access.
